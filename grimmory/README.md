# Home Assistant Application: Grimmory

A self-hosted library for your ebooks, comics and audiobooks.

Grimmory reads EPUB, PDF and comics in the browser, keeps shelves and reading
progress for everyone in the household, fills in metadata and covers, and syncs
with Kobo and KOReader devices. Books dropped into its BookDrop folder are
queued for review and filed into the library.
